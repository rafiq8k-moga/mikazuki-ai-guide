# Emotion Images Creator Permission

Mikazuki AI features **Emotion Images** (character face expressions shown inline in chat). All expression images available on the platform are **legally obtained and officially permitted by their creators**.

## Image Source

All expression images used come from the community project:

**[Kivotos Unofficial Expressions](https://kivotos-unofficial-expressions.pages.dev/)**

This project collects character face expressions (sprite expressions) created by volunteer fan-art creators.

## Permission Obtained

**Rafiq8K Studio**, the operator of Mikazuki AI, has **personally contacted (DM'd) the project owners**, and we **have received their permission** to use these images within Mikazuki AI.

## Terms of Use

The permission was granted under one main condition:

> **The expression images must NOT be placed behind a paywall.**

This means:

- The Emotion Images feature is available to **all users, free of charge** — both free and VIP users.
- Expression images will **never** become a paid-exclusive feature.
- Creator credit is displayed on every image (a small watermark linked to this page).

## Credit

Every expression image in chat shows the creator's name as credit. If you are a creator involved in the project and would like your artwork removed or your credit corrected, please contact us.
