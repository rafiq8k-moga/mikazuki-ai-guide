# Your Characters

Not ready to stop at the built-in characters? Mikazuki AI opens up two paths to have characters of your own: the **Local System** (characters stored on your device) and the **Cloud System** (characters stored in our database and enjoyed by everyone). This page walks through the creation form, the difference between the two systems, how to submit a character through **Character Requests**, and how to edit your work after it has been sent.

## Creating Your Own Character

Everything starts on the **"Create Character"** page with the subtitle *"Choose a character creation system."* Once you pick a system, you enter the same form for both paths. At the very top sits **"Character Photo"**: pick an image and adjust the crop area in the *"Adjust Image"* dialog — **"1:1 ratio, a clearly visible face is recommended."** Then the **"Basic Details"** section holds Name, Age, Height, Weight, Likes, and Dislikes. In the Cloud flow, only the Name and Short Description are shown publicly, while *"Age, height, weight, likes, and dislikes are used for internal moderation only - they are not shown publicly. Only the Name & Short Description are displayed."* The **"Short Description"** itself only exists in the Cloud flow (up to 255 characters, *"Shown publicly on the character card & page (max 255 characters)."*).

The **"Personality"** section is the heart of the character: describe the personality, background, and way of speaking in up to 10,000 characters. The guidance is written right there: personality covers *"name, age, persona, background, speaking style, habits, likes, and dislikes. The more complete it is, the more consistent the character will be in chat."* Then come the **"Chat Examples"**, which have two modes: **Advanced** (write freely with the `{{user}}:` and `{{char}}:` markers, separate each conversation with a blank line, and the **"Add template"** button is ready to help) and **Guided** (your message ↔ the character's reply, one pair at a time). Validation is fairly strict — the **"Continue"** button only lights up when the required fields are filled, with the reminder *"Please complete: {fields}"*.

The app explains *how chat examples work* in a sentence worth quoting in full: *"Chat examples work as real examples (few-shot) so the AI imitates your character's speaking style and personality. Write them like a real chat: the user's message first, then the character's reply. The more consistent examples you give, the better the character replies."* So these are not just extra description — the examples are the direct model for how the AI speaks. One sweet detail: **your draft saves automatically on this device** while you type, so moving to another page or losing your signal does not wipe out your work.

## Local vs Cloud

The choice page shows both systems side by side with honest details. **Local System** — *"Characters are stored on your device"* — its strengths are *"Only you can access your characters"*, *"Privacy is preserved"*, and *"Freedom to imagine"*; the trade-off is *"Stored locally (lost if not backed up, good for privacy)"*, and local characters **have no Bond system** and **cannot be used in [Group Chat](/en/fitur/group-chat)**. Right now the Local option is labeled *"Coming soon..."* — the form and preview already exist, but the storage itself has not opened yet. The **Cloud System** — *"Characters are stored in our database"* — can use the [Bond](/en/fitur/bond-calendar) system and Group Chat, and is *"Verified in our database"*; in exchange, cloud characters are *"Accessible to everyone (great if you like sharing characters, less great for character privacy)"* and the check *"Review uses AI Safety plus manual checks, adding a character to the database can take 1-7 business days"*.

The Cloud flow ends at the **"Cloud Character Preview"** page: re-check all the data (including the **"Character ID"** generated from your character's name) while the safety check runs in the background — its status reads *"Checking content safety..."*. AI Safety comes in tiers, from a light note all the way to a refusal: content that breaks the guidelines cannot be submitted (*"Rejected — This is not a false positive. Edit your content."*), and severe violations carry a clear consequence — *"1 hour cooldown — You are blocked from the Create/Edit Cloud feature for 60 minutes."* One prohibition is written in both flows: *"Creating child characters for NSFW use is prohibited by the system (AI Safety Check)"*.

Press **"Save"** and your character joins the review queue: *"The character will be reviewed manually within 1-3 business days. Once accepted, we will notify you via Email and in-app Notification. Accepted characters will be placed in the 'Fan Creation' category."* There are submission limits too: you cannot send a new character while you *"still have characters awaiting review"*, and *"Daily submission limit reached. Try again tomorrow."* shows up when the daily quota is used up.

## Character Requests

If you just want a certain character made for you — rather than writing it yourself — open the **"Character Requests"** page through the `/requests` menu. The only field to fill in is **"Character Name *"** (maximum 100 characters) in the **"Submit New Character"** form; press **"Submit Request"** and note the hint: *"Manual review takes 1-3 days. Bots are not auto-created."* — requests are approved by a human, not a machine.

Requests are paginated against spam: 5 requests per 12 hours, with full transparency — *"Anti-spam limit: 5 requests per 12 hours (IP and account recorded). Names are normalized; duplicates are allowed but have lower priority."* Two more things worth knowing: deleting a request *"does not restore the 12-hour quota"*, and submissions can be temporarily disabled by an admin (*"Character submissions are temporarily closed."* — *"Please check Discord for updates."*). Your request history appears on the same page with the status **pending**, **approved**, **silent_approved** (accepted without an announcement), or **rejected** along with the reason. As the footer reminds you: processed requests are deleted automatically 30 days after being handled, pending requests are kept, and IP addresses are recorded for moderation purposes.

## Editing

All of your cloud work is gathered on the **"My Characters"** page, complete with the counts *"{count} pending / approved / rejected"* and the note *"Every fan creation is reviewed manually (1-3 days). You'll receive an email once a decision is made."* For **Pending** or **Rejected** submissions, the **"Edit"** button reveals *"Previous rejection reason:"* and then opens the full form to fix and resubmit for review (*"Fix the character details and resubmit for review."*). Pending submissions can also be pulled back with **"Withdraw"** — the confirmation is firm: *"Withdraw the submission for "{name}"? This can't be undone."*

Characters that are already **Approved** appear as public characters: their page carries the name, the category (now **"Fan Creation"**), the description, the seven *Personality* lines (Nickname, Age, Nationality, Height, Weight, Likes, Dislikes), a **"Start Chat"** button — which turns into *"Continue chat"* after you recruit them — plus the **Translate** toggle and the 🎂 cake icon on their birthday. Click **"Edit"** there (or on the My Characters page) to revise a character that is already live. It is very different from editing a submission: *"Changes go live immediately. The image is not changed"* — no re-review, no photo replacement, and of the Basic Details only the Name can be touched, besides the Description, Personality, and Chat Examples. The reminder on its preview page makes your responsibility clear: *"These changes go live immediately without review. Make sure they follow the community guidelines."*
