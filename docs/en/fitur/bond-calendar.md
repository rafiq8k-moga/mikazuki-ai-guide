# Bond & Calendar

Every interaction with a character builds a **bond** — a kind of closeness whose size you can actually see. The bond appears as a **heart with a number** on the right side of each character chat row (not groups), and it grows as the two of you talk. Its companion page, the **Calendar**, helps you memorize the important dates around your characters — above all their birthdays — so no moment slips by.

## What Is a Bond

The bond with each character is tiered like a level: the number inside the heart in the chat list is your bond level with that character, while the heart's color shows the status of your relationship. Open the character detail panel in a chat, and you will see a bond chip like **"Lv. 12 — Close Friend"** complete with a progress bar toward the next level. You can also sort the chat list by bond through the **Bond** option on the [Basic Chat](/en/fitur/basic-chat) page, so the closest characters are always the closest to your fingertips.

Bond XP increases every time the two of you interact; the higher the level, the more XP is needed to climb again. There are also moments when a character gives more XP than usual — depending on how pleasant the conversation feels from their point of view. Conversely, hurtful conversations can reduce XP, and if the relationship falls too deep, the status can deteriorate.

## Recruiting & Choices

A bond is born as soon as you start chatting with a character — sometimes through an action button on the character's page that "recruits" them into your chat list, sometimes automatically on the first message. Successfully recruited characters also count on your bonds list on the Profile page. From there, **the choices within your conversation that steer the relationship** are what matter, not special buttons: how you treat the character, the topics you raise, and the decisions you make in scenes are all judged.

The relationship has two big moments triggered through conversation. At **level 50**, you can go through the confession moment: accepted, the relationship becomes lovers; declined, the status becomes rejected. At **level 100**, the commitment moment opens — the character can accept your proposal so you become husband/wife (it can happen directly from a normal relationship too), or the two of you agree to keep dating. When the status changes, a notification like *"Your relationship has improved. Current status: Lover."* appears in the chat — and for relationships rising to a romantic stage or the cherished one, a rain of hearts flies across the screen to welcome it.

Not all changes go up. The "Rejected" and "Stuck in Dating" statuses are **final decisions** that cannot be undone through conversation, while the **Enemy** status still has a way home: be kind again through conversations and apologies, and once the bond level is high enough, the good news appears — *"Relationship is healing."*

## Relationship Levels

The label reflects a combination of status and level. On the normal path, the labels form a ladder: **Acquaintance** (level 5+), **Friend** (10+), **Close Friend** (20+), **Best Friend** (30+), **Soulmate (Friendship)** (40+), and **Crush** after passing level 50. From the big moments above, the label can change into **Lover**, **Husband/Wife**, **Rejected**, or **Enemy**.

The heart color, the bond chip, and the label are consistent per status, so once memorized you can read a relationship at a single glance:

| Status | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Normal, Kesayangan (Favorite) | Blue |
| Lover | Pink |
| Stuck in Dating | Orange |
| Husband/Wife | Purple |
| Enemy | Red |
| Rejected | Gray |

"Lover" and "Stuck in Dating" both display as the label "Lover" — what distinguishes them is the decision at the commitment moment: keeping on dating makes the heart's color turn orange.

Child characters have a path of their own: the relationship grows toward the **Kesayangan (Favorite)** status, and every romantic moment is always replaced with a family version. Adult characters who dream of this special label will not get it — Kesayangan (Favorite) is exclusive to child characters.

## Branch Paths: The Story After Marriage

Bond levels now cap at **400** — and marriage is no longer the end of the story. Once you are officially married, a **branch path** opens: a series of special moments triggered naturally through conversation. There is no button to press — just live the story, and when the moment is right, the character marks it themselves. Each completed moment grants bonus XP (with a 12-hour cooldown per moment, so it stays special), and your relationship label climbs beyond "Spouse":

| Stage | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Spouse (Suami/Istri) |
| 2 | Pasangan Sejati (True Partner) |
| 3 | Belahan Jihi (Soulmate) |
| 4 | Takdir (Destiny) |
| 5 | Kekal (Eternal) |
| 6 | Ikatan Akhir (Final Bond) |

The moments include your **wedding anniversary**, **journeys together**, **renewing your vows**, **sacrifices**, **legacy**, and the **eternal vow** — culminating in the **Final Bond**, which only unlocks at level 400. Meanwhile, child characters have their own family path: warm moments like comforting, small adventures, and family dinners raise their label from **Kesayangan (Favorite)** to **Sahabat Sejati (True Friend)**, **Keluarga Kecil (Little Family)**, and finally **Tak Terpisahkan (Inseparable)**.

You can track all of these moments from the **Bond** tab on the [Character Data and Memories](/en/fitur/character-data) page: an RPG-style relationship ladder showing the stages you have completed, the next stage and its requirement, and the ones still locked.

## Resetting a Bond

Want to start a character's story over from scratch? Bond reset now lives in **Advanced delete** (the chat list's selection mode, or the three-dot menu in the chat panel). The option is always visible, but it can only be checked once both **"Delete local chat"** and the **server summary deletion** are checked too — making sure the story truly starts on a blank page rather than just erasing the bond. After a reset, the entire branch history is gone and the bond is rebuilt from zero.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** page has three tabs: **Calendar**, **Birthday**, and **Event**. The Calendar tab shows a monthly grid with a small red dot under each date that has an entry; tap the date to see the list of character birthdays that day, each entry marked with a cake icon and the label *"Character Birthday"*. An **"Upcoming 30 days"** list sums up the meetings ahead, for example `15 Nov · 13 days to go` — and when the date falls today, it reads exactly *"today"*.

Birthday markers don't stop at the Calendar page. When a character's big day arrives, a **cake emoji 🎂 appears beside their name on the chat row** (see [Basic Chat](/en/fitur/basic-chat)), so you know right away to head there first to say happy birthday. Both use the same schedule and are updated automatically from character data.

In the **Birthday** and **Event** tabs appears a list that can be filtered through search (*"Search schedule..."*); when the results are empty, the **"Reset filters"** button restores the list. Calendar data is temporarily cached on the server (refreshed every 24 hours) so the page opens quickly.

## Import from HaloStory AI

If you are moving over from HaloStory AI, your old bonds don't need to start from zero. In the initial setup wizard, choose the **migration** path, sign in and verify your HaloStory AI account (via an email verification code), then after you approve the data preview that is shown, you arrive at the **"Import your bonds"** step — *"Transfer your progress with each character from HaloStory AI."*

Press the **"Import bonds"** button and wait a moment. The result is reported as it is: for example **"12 bonds imported."** — your XP, level, and relationship status are carried over intact. Characters not yet available in Mikazuki AI are skipped with a note like **"3 skipped (character not available yet)."**, and if your old account turns out to have no bonds: *"No bonds found on your old account. You may continue."* When HaloStory data cannot be reached, the message is honest: *"HaloStory data cannot be reached right now. Please try again later."*

If a certain bond status from the old data is no longer recognized in the new system, that value is safely normalized into a normal bond — it does not cancel the whole import. This import only runs in the initial wizard; once you have completed it, keep chatting and let your old bonds live again.
